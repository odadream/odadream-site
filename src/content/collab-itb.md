## Innovation Ballet Theatre

Equal co-production partner for ODA.dream's Kaluga neuro-theatre line: full-evening **[[schrodinger|Schrödinger]]** and **[[interference|Interference of Realities]]**, staged with the company at [[org-ikc|ICC]] and premiered at the **[[event-tsiolkovsky|Tsiolkovsky Festival]]**.

Institutional host context → [[org-itb|org card]]. Timeline from [[event-kod-provincii|Code of Province]] (2024) through the [[event-itb-lab-2024-11|2024 lab]] to festival editions 2025–2026.

Stub — expand: choreography (Ksenia Golyzhibina), music (Alexander Shesternin), touring rider. INFO-DEBTS DEBT-010.
