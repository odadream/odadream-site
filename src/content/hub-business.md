Some come for the network. Some for the content.  
Those who are remembered — came for the experience that cannot be captured in a deck.

_ODA.dream is that experience._

---

**When the event should say something important about your company**

There are hub-events that entertain. And there are hub-events that leave an impression — because something genuinely meaningful happened.

Inviting ODA.dream is a decision that speaks before anyone says a word. It signals that your company pays attention to what is actually important: human consciousness, the future of technology, the depth behind innovation.

## What this looks like in practice

Participants put on EEG neurointerfaces. In real time, the screen shows their own brain activity transformed into generative visual art. They see — perhaps for the first time — what their attention and inner state look like as an image.

This is not a party trick. It is a moment of genuine experience that most people remember for years.
