**Date:** 2026.02.23

The Lotus Navigator has been rebuilt from the ground up. Dragging now feels like touching water — the panel follows the finger without hesitation or lag. A small attention ritual: after a few seconds of stillness, the panel quietly peeks open and retreats. The arrow breathes until you respond.
